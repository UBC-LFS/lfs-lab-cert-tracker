campaign About LFS Access and Training Record System (LFS ATRS)

LFS Access and Training Record System (LFS ATRS) should provide a transparent and streamline process to fulfill safety training requirements while ensuring access requests can be processed and tracked properly. The LFS ATRS is built to fulfill the following:

  • Collect and keep record of everyone's UBC work related training records required for LFS students, staff and faculty members.
  • Ensure training requirements for each work area are up-to-date and fulfilled for the individual applying for key/fob/alarm request of the space.
  • Automate missing training and expired training reminders to ensure all training requirements are kept up to date as best as possible.
  • NEW Training requirements for each room in each LFS building will now need to be fulfilled and imported before the access request form becomes available for users.
  • NEW Access request form and its downstream approval process will be transparent to users, supervisors and administrators, who can monitor the progress of access requests all in one place.

Any questions regarding LFS ATRS can be directed to lfs.facilities@ubc.ca.
